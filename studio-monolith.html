The Studio

We build slowly, so what we build endures.

Studio Monolith is an independent practice of architects and interior designers. We take on a small number of homes each year, and shape every one around its site, its climate and the people who will live there.

Contextual Architecture
Every building begins with the land, its orientation, views and weather.
Honest Materials
Stone, timber and metal left to age with grace, never imitated.
Human-Centric Light
Daylight is drawn through each room to follow the rhythm of the day.
