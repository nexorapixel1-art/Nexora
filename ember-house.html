Our Philosophy

We cook with one ingredient above all: time, and fire.

Every plate at The Ember House begins at the hearth. We burn only aged white oak split by a cooper two valleys over, and finish delicate cuts over binchotan, the white charcoal that burns clean enough to taste nothing but the ingredient. There is no gas line in our kitchen.

Our menu moves with the forager’s basket: ramps in April, chanterelles by August, quince and juniper once the frost arrives. If it did not grow, graze or swim within a day’s drive, it does not reach the fire.

“Fire is the oldest recipe we have. Our work is simply to listen to it.”

Chef Elias Varga, Founder
